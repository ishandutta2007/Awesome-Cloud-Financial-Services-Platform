## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects

*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟

- **[Apache Fineract](https://github.com/apache/fineract)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/apache/fineract?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/apache/fineract/stargazers)  
  **Open-source core banking platform**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **Designed to create a cloud-ready core banking system** that enables digital financial services for **everyone, including the unbanked and underbanked** [citation:6][citation:13]. **Modern, cost-effective, highly connected banking infrastructure**. **Cloud-based, multi-tenant Spring enterprise Java application** with composable architecture. **Deployed in 500+ private sector institutions serving 20M+ individuals across 56 countries** including MFIs, SACCOs, banks, fintechs, and telcos. **Public sector deployments**: Mexico (2021), India (2020), Sierra Leone (2022) [citation:12]. **Version 1.13.0 released October 2025** [citation:13]. **The most widely deployed open-source core banking platform globally**. 🏦

- **[Mifos X](https://github.com/openMF/mifosx)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/openMF/mifosx?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/openMF/mifosx/stargazers)  
  **Reference web and mobile apps for Apache Fineract**, MPL-2.0 licensed. **Out-of-the-box ready-to-deploy solution** on top of Apache Fineract, providing **staff and customer-facing web and mobile applications** along with **reporting, payments orchestration, and other ancillary solutions** [citation:5]. **The Mifos Initiative** is a global 501(c)3 non-profit whose mission is to **scale the development and impact of inclusive fintech** through an open community developing core banking and payments infrastructure [citation:12]. **The practical application layer for Fineract deployments**. 📱

- **[Cyclos](https://github.com/cyclosproject/cyclos4)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/cyclosproject/cyclos4?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/cyclosproject/cyclos4/stargazers)  
  **Scalable and reliable free and open source banking solution**, AGPL-3.0 licensed. **Comes with a pack of powerful features** for banking business models. **Available as a free-to-use version and an Open Source version**, creating space for improvement and customization [citation:5]. **Supports a wider array of services** and **customer-centric** banking models. 🔄

- **[MyBANCO](https://github.com/MyBANCO/mybanco)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/MyBANCO/mybanco?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/MyBANCO/mybanco/stargazers)  
  **Free and open source core banking tool**, open-source. **One of the most popular and powerful open source core banking tools** [citation:5]. **Banks or any other banking business model can save money** by using this software. **Designed to be modular and extensible** for different banking workflows. 🏗️

- **[WSO2 Micro Integrator](https://github.com/wso2/product-mi-tooling)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/wso2/product-mi-tooling?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/wso2/product-mi-tooling/stargazers)

- **[Ballerine](https://github.com/ballerine-io/ballerine)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/ballerine-io/ballerine?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/ballerine-io/ballerine/stargazers)

- **[Maybe Finance](https://github.com/maybe-finance/maybe)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/maybe-finance/maybe?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/maybe-finance/maybe/stargazers)

- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/frappe/erpnext?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/stargazers)

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️
- **Core banking systems are not interchangeable with general financial software** — they handle deposits, loans, payments, and regulatory reporting. **Apache Fineract is production-proven** in 500+ institutions across 56 countries [citation:12], but requires **significant operational expertise** for production deployments.
- **nCino's Agentic AI banking** delivers measurable results — **97.5% reduction in document search time** and **41% growth in active users in 10 weeks** at ConnectOne Bank [citation:4]. However, **agentic AI in banking requires governance** — every AI-assisted action must be **auditable, explainable, and under the institution's control**.
- **Thought Machine Vault Core is cloud-agnostic** — banks choose their hosting provider, or use it as SaaS [citation:11]. **Tier 1 banks including JPMorgan Chase, Lloyds, and Standard Chartered** are among its customers and investors [citation:18].
- **Open-source core banking is not free** — Fineract and Mifos eliminate licensing costs, but **implementation, customization, compliance validation, and ongoing operations** require dedicated engineering resources. **WSO2 demonstrated this at Nib Bank**: zero licensing cost, but **internal team investment** was critical to success [citation:8].
- **Always validate regulatory compliance** with your specific jurisdiction's requirements before deploying any financial services platform. **Financial infrastructure demands the highest levels of security, availability, and auditability**. 🏦
